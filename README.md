## Business Problem

Wanjiku runs a fresh-produce stall at Gikomba Market and needs to understand her sales performance and stock levels to make better business decisions. Without proper sales tracking, she may struggle to identify her best-performing days, monitor product profitability, and determine when to restock. This project uses Python to analyse 14 days of sales data, compare weekday and weekend performance, classify daily sales, monitor stock levels, and identify products requiring restocking.

## How to Run

Open the notebook in Google Colab: [Open stall_analysis.ipynb in Colab](https://colab.research.google.com/github/Veekilonzi/plp-da-a1-gikomba-stall-analysis/blob/main/stall_analysis.ipynb)

## Top Three Findings

1. **Weekend performance:** Average weekend sales were KES 4,900, compared to weekday sales of KES 2,685, representing an 82.50% increase.

2. **Stock shortages:** Tomatoes require an additional 7 kg, while Sukuma requires an additional 6 kg to reach their reorder levels.

3. **Product profitability:** Avocado generates the highest profit per kilogram at KES 60, while the estimated total potential profit across current stock is KES 4,015.
